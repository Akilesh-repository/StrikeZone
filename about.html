Timeline

Our History

From a humble 12-lane alley to a massive entertainment complex, our journey is defined by a passion for fun.

1998

The Beginning

StrikeZone opens its doors as a traditional 12-lane bowling alley in the heart of the city. It quickly becomes the favorite local hangout spot for Friday night leagues.

2005

The Arcade Expansion

We acquired the adjacent building, adding our first 3,000 sq. ft. arcade and redemption center, transforming the alley into a dual-threat entertainment space.

2015

Cosmic Renovation

A multi-million dollar renovation upgraded our lanes to 32, added custom neon LED lighting, a massive laser tag arena, and our signature VIP sports bar.

Today

The Premier Destination

Today, we host over 50,000 games a year, continuously updating our tech and food menu to ensure StrikeZone remains the top entertainment venue in the state.
