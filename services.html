Got Questions?

Services FAQ

Do I need to reserve a lane in advance?

While walk-ins are always welcome, we highly recommend reserving a lane online in advance, especially on Friday nights, weekends, and holidays when we frequently sell out.

Can I bring outside food or cake for a party?

Outside food and drinks are strictly prohibited due to health and safety regulations. However, if you book an official Birthday Party package, you are allowed to bring in a store-bought cake or cupcakes.

Are bowling shoes required?

Yes, specific bowling shoes are required to protect our lane approaches and ensure your safety. You can bring your own approved bowling shoes, or rent a pair from us at the front desk.

What is the age limit for Laser Tag?

For safety reasons and to comfortably wear the vest equipment, all laser tag players must be at least 7 years old and 48 inches tall. Closed-toe shoes are also required in the arena.
