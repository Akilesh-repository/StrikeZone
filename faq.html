Frequently Asked Questions

While walk-ins are always welcome, we highly recommend reserving a lane online in advance, especially on Friday nights, weekends, and holidays when we frequently sell out. Walk-in wait times during peak hours can exceed 90 minutes.

Outside food and drinks are strictly prohibited due to health and safety regulations. We have a full-service restaurant and bar on-site. However, if you book an official Birthday Party package, you are allowed to bring in a store-bought cake or cupcakes.

Yes, specific bowling shoes are required to protect our lane approaches and ensure your safety. You can bring your own approved bowling shoes, or rent a pair from us at the front desk. Socks are also mandatory and available for purchase if forgotten.

For safety reasons and to comfortably wear the vest equipment, all laser tag players must be at least 7 years old and 48 inches tall. Closed-toe shoes are also strictly required in the arena at all times.

A maximum of 6 people are allowed per lane for safety and comfort. If your group is larger than 6, you will need to rent a second lane. Our system automatically links adjacent lanes so your whole group can bowl together.

All lane reservations are non-refundable within 24 hours of your scheduled time. If you need to cancel or reschedule prior to the 24-hour window, please contact our front desk, and we will gladly issue a venue credit for a future visit.
